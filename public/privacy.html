Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 3, 2026

Welcome to HezarfApp. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information when you use our application, particularly when authenticating via Google.

1. Information We Collect

When you log in or sign up using Google OAuth, we collect the following information from your Google account:

  • Your email address
  • Your basic profile information (First Name and Last Name)

If you register directly with an email and password, we collect the name, surname, email address, and securely hashed password you provide.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To authenticate you and keep your session secure.
  • To provide you with the core functionalities of the HezarfApp dashboard.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. Your data is stored securely using Supabase and is only accessed by our application to provide you with the requested services.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal information only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, please contact us.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at via our Contact page.
